# Project-specific protocol

Status: DRAFT — no approval implied. Version must match `project.json`.

Complete with the researcher; record applicable choices and justified non-applicability.

1. Research question, objectives, contribution and feasibility.
2. Study design, rationale, evidence streams and publication endpoint.
3. Population/context, intervention/exposure/comparator when relevant.
4. Outcomes/teaching objectives; primary, secondary and exploratory hierarchy.
5. Units, denominators, dependence and report/cohort/patient overlap.
6. Sources, access, search families, restrictions and exploratory/formal distinction.
7. Eligibility, ambiguity handling, independent review roles and decision authority.
8. Data dictionary, provenance, missingness, extraction and human verification.
9. Appraisal/risk-of-bias and certainty methods with applicable sources.
10. Mapping, harmonization, derivations and verification rules.
11. Analysis/synthesis plan, assumptions, uncertainty and conditional pooling.
12. Ethics, consent, privacy and institutional requirements for this actual project.
13. AI task scope, human decisions, contribution records and disclosure.
14. Phase applicability, gates, meaningful tests and amendment policy.
15. Reporting standards, target audience and publication QA.

Record human approval only after the actual decision, identifying the reviewed version
and files in `audit/approvals.jsonl`. Preserve former versions when amending.
