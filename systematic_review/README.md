# CAN-CCHD Systematic Review — Formal Review Workspace

This directory marks the start of the **new formal CAN-CCHD systematic review and meta-analysis**.

## Scientific premise

The review evaluates the clinical yield of newborn pulse-oximetry screening beyond CCHD-specific diagnostic accuracy. It operationalizes **Clinically Actionable Non-CCHD Conditions (CAN-CCHD)** to distinguish CCHD-negative failed screens that lead to clinically meaningful management from failed screens without actionable disease.

## Clean-start rule

The prior application-based workflow, database, searches, deduplication outputs, screening decisions, and test/mock data are **legacy exploratory work**.

They are not inherited into this formal review.

The new review will rebuild prospectively:

1. protocol;
2. search strategy;
3. formal searches and immutable raw exports;
4. collection QA;
5. deduplication;
6. title/abstract screening;
7. full-text eligibility;
8. extraction and dual human verification;
9. risk-of-bias assessment;
10. CAN-CCHD mapping;
11. statistical analysis;
12. Saudi Heart abstract;
13. full manuscript.
