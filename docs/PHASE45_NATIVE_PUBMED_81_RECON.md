## New primary inclusions

### NR007 — Williams et al. 2021

*Newborn Pulse Oximetry for Infants Born Out-of-Hospital* (PMID 34531289; DOI 10.1542/peds.2020-048785).

- 3,019 newborns; early 1–4 h and late 24–48 h screening.
- 3 CCHD detected.
- Twelve false-positive cases had other pathologies, including noncritical CHD, pulmonary disease or infection.
- **INCLUDE with denominator-convention flag** because field interpretation and strict algorithm interpretation differed.
- Do not automatically treat as a Miller 2016 companion; cohort overlap requires date/center verification.

### NR008 — Narayen et al. 2016

*Pulse Oximetry Screening for Critical Congenital Heart Disease after Home Birth and Early Discharge* (PMID 26746119; DOI 10.1016/j.jpeds.2015.12.004).

- 3,625 eligible births; 3,090 consented; 3,059 screened.
- No CCHD detected in the screening cohort; 32 false-positive screens.
- Secondary extraction describes respiratory disease, infection/sepsis, noncritical CHD, other pathology and healthy infants.
- **INCLUDE with numerator-discrepancy QA flag** because later summary percentages and category totals require primary-table reconciliation.
- Pilot Oct 2013–Oct 2014; later POLAR Jul 2015–Dec 2016: program lineage but no temporal overlap on current evidence.

### NR009 — Tekgündüz et al. 2021

*Oxygen saturation and perfusion index screening in neonates at high altitudes: can PDA be predicted?* (PMID 32504134; DOI 10.1007/s00431-020-03698-1).

- 501 neonates >35 weeks; 21 positive screening tests; no CCHD.
- 9 screen-positive infants had PDA.
- Remaining 12 CCHD-negative positives are not adequately characterized in the abstract.
- **INCLUDE with altitude, partial-ascertainment and PDA-actionability flags**. Uncharacterized infants remain `diagnosis not reported` unless full text resolves them.
