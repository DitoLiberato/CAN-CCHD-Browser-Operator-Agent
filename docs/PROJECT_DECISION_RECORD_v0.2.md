# Case Report 01 — Project Decision Record v0.2

**Date:** 2026-09-03  
**Status:** Phase 0 decisions updated after additional clinical evolution; patient-specific procedural details remain off public repository.

## Decisions carried forward

### 1. No first-association framing

The core anatomic association has direct published precedent. The manuscript must not use `first association`, `unique association`, or equivalent framing before formal evidence adjudication.

### 2. Review design retained

The review component remains a **systematic review of reported cases** because the direct published-case literature appears potentially enumerable and management sequencing is a central research question.

### 3. Primary direct-analog definition

The primary review set will consist of unique published human patients in whom both Tetralogy of Fallot and Morgagni diaphragmatic hernia are documented.

Trisomy 21 is a comparison/modifier axis, not an inclusion requirement.

### 4. Contextual evidence remains separate

Broader management analogs may include other congenital diaphragmatic hernias with TOF, transcatheter RVOT bridge strategies, trisomy-21/Morgagni diagnostic literature and relevant surgical/physiologic evidence.

These papers must not be counted as direct TOF–Morgagni cases.

### 5. Management novelty hypothesis refined

Additional clinical evolution materially shifted the scientific emphasis away from a precommitted device strategy toward **response-guided transcatheter palliation and bridge-to-growth decision making**.

The public branch records this only as a methodological hypothesis. Detailed patient-level procedural facts remain in the private Case Core.

The formal review must therefore determine whether prior direct or contextual cases report:

- balloon-based RVOT palliation;
- RVOT stenting;
- surgical shunts or alternative palliation;
- response-guided escalation/de-escalation during catheterization;
- successful stabilization without the originally contemplated device;
- later definitive cardiac/diaphragmatic repair after growth.

### 6. Case maturity decision

The case has an early post-intervention endpoint sufficient to justify continuing the project.

However, the Case Core is not ready to freeze until key primary documentation and an early discharge/follow-up endpoint are obtained or explicitly declared unavailable.

### 7. Imaging strategy

Multimodality imaging is available privately. Candidate manuscript use includes chest radiography, thoracic CT, echocardiography and angiography.

Image publication is blocked until guardian consent, de-identification review and final teaching-point selection.

### 8. Publication endpoint remains flexible

Two defensible endpoints remain possible:

**A. Early bridge-to-growth report** — if the child remains clinically stable after discharge and the manuscript is completed before definitive surgery.

**B. Full staged-course report** — if definitive cardiac/diaphragmatic surgery and postoperative outcome become available within a reasonable manuscript timeline.

The endpoint should be chosen prospectively before Case Core freeze rather than retrofitted after writing.

### 9. Privacy decision reaffirmed

The parent repository is public. Detailed patient-level data, source PDFs and images remain off-repository until explicit consent/privacy clearance.

### 10. Current project decision

**CONTINUE.**

Proceed with Phase 0 protocol approval and Phase 0.5 private Case Core reconstruction. Formal literature collection begins only after Gate 0 is locked.
