## Binding interpretation

`INCLUDE_PRIMARY` means the report passes report-level Phase 4 eligibility and proceeds to structured extraction. It does **not** mean its raw numerator/denominator can be pooled without the listed target-definition, actionability, missingness, timing, altitude, or overlap QA.

`COMPANION_NONINDEPENDENT` reports remain in provenance/citation tables but cannot create a second cohort weight.

`CONDITIONAL_SUPPORTING` (NR001) is not a population screening denominator and is not pooled in the primary proportion.
