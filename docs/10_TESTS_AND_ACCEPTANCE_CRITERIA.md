## Final Acceptance Criteria
The pipeline is acceptable when it starts with Research Plan; phases are gated; source searches are logged query-by-query; records retain source/query provenance; deduplication supports exact bulk merge with confirmation; uncertain duplicates require review; title/abstract screening is sensitive; full-text retrieval does not treat no free PDF as exclusion; extraction occurs only after full-text eligibility; AI extraction requires human verification; case reports are excluded from quantitative synthesis; QA blocks unsafe analysis; analysis uses the protocol-defined denominator and outcome hierarchy; drafts are generated only from verified data; all actions are auditable; and Phase 11 cannot produce a release candidate until the Publication QA Canon has been satisfied or every exception is explicitly documented.

A publication-ready full manuscript must additionally demonstrate: verified authorship/affiliations and declarations; publication-native terminology; explicit primary/secondary outcome hierarchy; reproducible search reporting; accurate AI/human-control disclosure; reference order by actual first appearance; verified reference-to-claim support; zero orphan references; complete citation coverage of every study displayed in tables/figures; an analytic-unit-to-publication crosswalk; correct companion/overlap handling; numerical reconciliation to frozen analysis; and visual QA of final rendered artifacts.
