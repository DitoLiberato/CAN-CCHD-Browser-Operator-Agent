# Phase 4 tranche R016–R025

Date: 2026-08-21
Branch: `phase4-consolidation`

See `PHASE4_CONSOLIDATION_LEDGER_v0.1.md` for governance and global counts.

R016 EXCLUDE PRIMARY — Gómez-Rodríguez 2015 Mexico. 1,037 term newborns screened; 2 CCHD detected and test-accuracy metrics imply CCHD false positives, while 10 minor CHD are reported in the overall study population. However, the report available does not provide a defensible diagnosis/outcome/no-diagnosis classification specifically among the CCHD-negative failed pulse-ox screens. Criterion 6 not satisfied for CAN-CCHD quantitative synthesis.

R017 INCLUDE / HIGHLY INFORMATIVE — Jawin 2015 Malaysia. 5,247 apparently healthy term newborns screened pre-discharge; 15 positive screens. Two had CCHD. All 13 CCHD-negative positive screens had clinically significant non-cardiac hypoxemic disease requiring hospitalisation/treatment: sepsis n=2 and respiratory disease n=11. Primary denominator=13; CAN-CCHD=13.

R018 INCLUDE / TRANSITIONAL-NONACTIONABLE FLAG — Özalkaya 2016 Bursa, Turkey. 8,208 screened after 24 h; 7 positive pulse-ox screens; 6 CCHD and 1 CCHD-negative positive. Echocardiography classified the single false positive as patent foramen ovale (PFO). Primary denominator=1; PFO should be coded transitional/non-actionable unless frozen actionability rules specify otherwise.

R019 INCLUDE — Van Niekerk 2016 POPSICLe, South Africa. Two babies failed screening and underwent echocardiography; one CCHD and the other neonatal sepsis. Primary CCHD-negative failed denominator=1; CAN-CCHD sepsis=1.

R020 INCLUDE / HIGHLY INFORMATIVE — POLAR study 2018 Netherlands. 23,959 screened in home-birth/early-discharge setting. Pulse oximetry was false positive for CCHD in 221 infants; 134 (61%) had non-cardiac illnesses, including infections n=31 and respiratory pathology n=88. Primary denominator=221; actionable non-cardiac numerator at least 134. Remaining disease subcategories and 87 without reported non-cardiac illness should be preserved without assuming healthy unless full text explicitly classifies them.

R021 INCLUDE / LESION-CLASSIFICATION FLAG — Miranda Peralta Panama. 2,236 healthy asymptomatic newborns; 16 positive screens. Echo: 6 structurally normal hearts, 6 PDA, 3 anomalous pulmonary venous connections, 1 complex critical right-heart lesion/CCHD. Primary CCHD-negative failed denominator=15. Actionability of PDA and the exact type/criticality of anomalous pulmonary venous connections must be frozen lesion-by-lesion before numerator coding; six are explicitly structurally normal on echo but not automatically clinically healthy beyond cardiac evaluation.

R022 INCLUDE / INCOMPLETE-ASCERTAINMENT + NONACTIONABLE FLAG — Soto Torselli 2020 Guatemala. 376 clinically healthy newborns, American screening algorithm. Final 11 positive screens; 10 echocardiograms completed and one participant lost to echo follow-up. Of 10 evaluated positives, 1 structurally normal and 9 showed acyanotic/noncritical cardiac findings, dominated by PFO, small ASD/PDA and two other anomalies (biventricular hypertrophy, dilated coronary sinus). Authors explicitly report no important haemodynamic or clinical compromise. No CCHD identified. Primary denominator=11; 10 have cardiac outcome data, 1 diagnosis not ascertained. Do not count PFO/minor lesions as actionable without frozen lesion rule.

R023 INCLUDE / HIGHLY INFORMATIVE — El Idrissi Slitine 2020 Morocco. 8,013 asymptomatic newborns screened; 15 failed. Five CCHD, five non-critical CHD and five study-defined false positives. The five false positives were 1 PPHN, 2 sepsis and 2 normal; noncritical CHD included AV canal, large ASD, hypertrophic cardiomyopathy and single atrium. Primary CCHD-negative failed denominator=10. At minimum PPHN+sepsis=3 actionable; lesion-level actionability of five noncritical cardiac diagnoses must be frozen.

R024 INCLUDE / HIGHLY INFORMATIVE — Gopalakrishnan 2021 India. 1,855 asymptomatic intramural neonates screened after 24 h. Sixteen screen positive: 3 CCHD and 13 CCHD-negative. Other conditions among all 13 CCHD-negative positives were 8 early-onset sepsis/congenital pneumonia, 2 PPHN and 3 transitional circulation. Primary denominator=13; CAN-CCHD actionable=10; transitional/non-actionable=3.

R025 INCLUDE / CLEAN OUTCOME — Flórez-Muñoz 2021 Colombia. 438 term asymptomatic newborns screened at 18–48 h; 4 positive. One TGA/CCHD, one moderate pulmonary hypertension, two healthy and discharged. Primary CCHD-negative failed denominator=3; CAN-CCHD=1 (pulmonary hypertension); explicitly healthy/no actionable diagnosis=2.
