# Case Report 01 — Project Decision Record v0.1

**Date:** 2026-09-03  
**Status:** Phase 0 decisions recorded; case-specific clinical details intentionally retained off public repository.

## Decisions made after initial case review

### 1. The central anatomic association is not presumed novel

A preliminary literature probe identified direct published precedent for the core association, including precedent that overlaps the relevant syndromic context.

Therefore:

- do not build the article around a `first association` claim;
- do not use `unique` before the formal review;
- novelty must be adjudicated from presentation, diagnostic reasoning, physiology, management sequence, intervention choice and/or outcome.

### 2. Review format

Provisional design selected:

**Systematic review of reported cases**, embedded in the case report.

Rationale:

- the direct reported-case literature appears small enough to enumerate;
- formal enumeration is more defensible for novelty than a narrative convenience sample;
- sequencing of interventions across prior cases may be clinically more informative than an association-only review.

### 3. Primary review emphasis

The review should prioritize:

- presentation and diagnostic pathway;
- competing physiologic contributors;
- intervention sequence;
- cardiac-first, noncardiac-first, simultaneous, or staged strategies;
- response after each stage;
- final outcome and follow-up.

Syndromic status is a planned comparison/modifier axis rather than a mandatory inclusion criterion unless Phase 0 is later amended.

### 4. Case completeness requirement

The currently available clinical source describes an intended staged strategy. A plan is not an outcome.

Before the final manuscript claims that the staged strategy was useful/successful, primary clinical documentation must verify as applicable:

- procedure actually performed;
- technical result;
- clinical response;
- subsequent intervention(s);
- complications;
- discharge/follow-up endpoint.

### 5. Privacy decision

Because the parent repository is public, the detailed Case Core and source documents remain off-repository until explicit privacy/consent clearance. The public branch contains only methodology, de-identification-safe templates, and literature-review artifacts.

### 6. Current viability assessment

**Decision: CONTINUE.**

The case appears potentially publishable, but the final contribution cannot yet be frozen. The decisive information will be the completed clinical course and the formal reported-case review.
