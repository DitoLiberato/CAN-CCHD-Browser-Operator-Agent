# Case Report 01 — Phase 0 Case–Review Protocol v0.1

**Date:** 2026-09-03  
**Branch:** `case-report-01`  
**Status:** **DRAFT FOR PHYSICIAN APPROVAL — NOT YET LOCKED**

## 1. Article design

Planned article:

> **Case report with an embedded systematic review of reported cases**

The index case and literature review are independent evidence streams until Phase 7 Dual Freeze.

The index patient’s detailed chronology, source documents and procedure-specific outcome remain private/off the public repository pending consent/privacy clearance.

## 2. Clinical teaching problem

The article will address a complex cardiorespiratory management problem in which congenital cardiac obstruction and a concomitant diaphragmatic lesion may both contribute to hypoxemia/respiratory instability.

The article must avoid single-cause attribution unless the longitudinal clinical course supports it.

The intended teaching value is not the mere coexistence of diagnoses, but the diagnostic and management reasoning required when:

- cyanosis and respiratory failure have multiple plausible contributors;
- cardiac palliation, diaphragmatic surgery and definitive intracardiac repair compete for timing;
- a transcatheter intervention may be used as a bridge to growth/definitive surgery;
- the definitive transcatheter endpoint may be response-guided rather than precommitted.

## 3. Provisional novelty hypotheses

These are hypotheses to test, not manuscript claims:

1. The central anatomic association itself is **not presumed novel**.
2. Potential novelty may lie in the clinical presentation, competing physiology, diagnostic pathway, sequencing of interventions, response-guided catheter strategy, avoidance/escalation of a planned device-based palliation, growth strategy and/or later combined surgical management.
3. Any `first`, `unique`, or equivalent language is prohibited before Phase 10 novelty adjudication.

## 4. Primary systematic-review question

> **Among published human cases in which Tetralogy of Fallot (TOF) and Morgagni diaphragmatic hernia coexist in the same patient, what clinical presentation, diagnostic pathway, intervention sequence, cardiac palliation strategy, hernia management, definitive repair strategy and outcomes have been reported?**

## 5. Secondary review questions

1. How often is a syndromic diagnosis reported, particularly trisomy 21, among direct published analogs?
2. Which lesion was treated first: cardiac, diaphragmatic, simultaneous/combined, or staged?
3. What bridge strategies were used before definitive TOF repair?
4. Were RVOT balloon dilation/valvuloplasty, RVOT stenting, ductal stenting, systemic-to-pulmonary shunt or other palliation reported?
5. What rationale did authors provide for their intervention sequence?
6. What short- and longer-term responses/complications were reported after each stage?
7. Did respiratory symptoms, poor acoustic windows, infection, airway disease, or other competing explanations contribute to diagnostic delay or physiologic uncertainty?

## 6. Primary-set eligibility

### Include

Original human reports/series that:

1. describe at least one patient with TOF;
2. document a Morgagni diaphragmatic hernia in the same patient;
3. provide original patient-level or extractable case-level clinical data;
4. report at least one clinically relevant domain such as presentation, diagnosis, intervention, sequencing or outcome.

Eligible publication forms may include:

- case report;
- case series;
- cohort/registry report if an eligible individual case can be reliably identified and extracted;
- letter/brief report with original case data.

No date or language restriction is planned initially.

### Exclude from the primary direct-analog set

- TOF without Morgagni hernia;
- Morgagni hernia without TOF;
- diaphragmatic hernia type not identifiable as Morgagni;
- review/editorial/commentary without original case data;
- animal/non-human report;
- publication that duplicates the same patient without adding unique usable information, after report-to-patient reconciliation.

Duplicate publications describing one patient will be linked rather than counted as independent cases.

## 7. Secondary contextual evidence

Evidence outside the direct primary set may be retained with an explicit role, including:

- TOF with other congenital diaphragmatic hernia types when management sequencing is highly informative;
- transcatheter RVOT palliation used as a bridge to growth or noncardiac surgery;
- pediatric Morgagni hernia in trisomy 21 for diagnostic/contextual comparison;
- literature on cardiopulmonary interaction, airway/respiratory confounding, imaging limitations or combined surgical strategies.

Secondary-context papers must never be counted as direct TOF–Morgagni cases.

## 8. Pre-specified comparison axes

For each unique direct reported patient, extract when available:

- age and sex;
- weight/size at relevant intervention;
- syndromic status;
- TOF anatomy/severity and pulmonary artery anatomy;
- hernia anatomy/content/physiologic effect;
- presentation;
- cyanosis/hypoxemia severity;
- respiratory failure or airway disease;
- infection/sepsis or other competing physiology;
- diagnostic timing and imaging modality;
- sequence of interventions;
- cardiac-first vs hernia-first vs simultaneous/combined vs other staged strategy;
- transcatheter palliation type and rationale;
- response after each intervention;
- complications;
- definitive cardiac repair;
- diaphragmatic repair;
- follow-up/outcome;
- authors’ stated teaching point.

Missing is missing; unreported is not negative.

## 9. Search framework

Phase 1 must use high-recall search families rather than one narrow query.

Minimum concepts:

- `Tetralogy of Fallot` / `TOF` / historical variants;
- `Morgagni hernia` / `Morgagni-Larrey` / anterior diaphragmatic hernia variants;
- relevant syndromic/context terms only as sensitivity expansions, not mandatory terms;
- management concepts such as palliation, RVOT, balloon dilation/valvuloplasty, stent, shunt, repair and staged surgery as supplementary searches.

Minimum reproducible public sources:

- PubMed/MEDLINE;
- Europe PMC;
- Crossref/OpenAlex for metadata/citation support.

Supplementary as useful/accessible:

- Google Scholar;
- backward and forward citation chasing;
- Scopus/Web of Science/Embase if available;
- regional databases if signal warrants.

Known direct-precedent papers found in the preliminary probe will be designated **sentinel recall records** during Phase 1. Search closure is blocked if the calibrated strategy fails to recover them without explanation.

## 10. Review synthesis rule

The direct reported-case corpus is a selected literature with no population denominator.

Therefore the review may report:

- counts of unique published cases;
- descriptive distributions/characteristics when meaningful;
- intervention sequences;
- qualitative similarities/differences;
- individual outcomes.

It must not infer prevalence, incidence or probability of the association or of a management strategy from published-case counts.

## 11. Reporting/appraisal framework

Planned standards:

- CARE for the index case;
- PRISMA 2020 for the systematic review component if the systematic label is retained;
- JBI case-report/case-series critical-appraisal tools as appropriate to the included report type.

Appraisal findings will be descriptive/domain-based. No invented aggregate numeric quality score will be used unless explicitly supported by the selected framework.

## 12. Case-source requirements before final Case Core freeze

The private Case Stream should obtain, when available:

- full catheterization report;
- procedure measurements and device/balloon details;
- angiographic pre/post imaging;
- contemporaneous physiologic measurements;
- relevant co-interventions around the procedure;
- post-procedure ward/discharge documentation;
- early follow-up and recurrence/non-recurrence of major symptoms;
- growth trajectory;
- definitive surgical documentation and outcome if reached before manuscript freeze.

The article may proceed before definitive surgery only if the chosen publication endpoint is explicitly limited to an early bridging outcome and that limitation is scientifically justified.

## 13. Imaging plan

Subject to consent/privacy clearance, candidate figures include:

- chest radiography;
- thoracic CT defining the diaphragmatic lesion and cardiopulmonary anatomy;
- echocardiography, with explicit acknowledgement of technical/acoustic limitations;
- angiographic pre/post transcatheter comparison;
- CARE-style clinical timeline.

Image selection must prioritize teaching value and de-identification.

## 14. Consent, ethics and privacy

Before any patient-level manuscript material or clinical image is committed publicly/submitted:

- guardian publication consent must be documented;
- institutional requirements for case-report ethics/IRB determination must be checked and documented;
- all images/text must undergo de-identification review;
- repository-publication policy must be revisited.

Consent/ethics status does not block literature searching, but it blocks public release/submission of identifiable or potentially re-identifiable case material.

## 15. Amendment rules

After Phase 0 lock, changes to:

- direct-analog eligibility;
- primary review question;
- primary vs contextual evidence boundary;
- comparison axes with potential to bias novelty adjudication;
- systematic-review label/process;

require an explicit `PHASE0_PROTOCOL_AMENDMENT` with rationale and date.
