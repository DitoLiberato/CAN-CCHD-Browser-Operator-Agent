# Phase 4 tranche — R086–R096

Status: adjudicated and ready to merge into the canonical Phase 4 ledger.
Date: 2026-08-21

## Decisions

| Corpus ID | Report | Phase 4 decision | Key note |
|---|---|---|---|
| R086 | Garg 2013 — New Jersey statewide program | INCLUDE | Canonical ID assigned to previously adjudicated report. Statewide program with 49 failed screens; among 30 infants whose evaluation was triggered solely by POX, 3 had CCHD and 17 had other diagnoses/findings. Retain detailed extraction and check denominator framing at study-level synthesis. |
| R087 | Kochilas 2013 — Minnesota implementation | INCLUDE | 7,549 screened; 6 reported failed screens; 1 CCHD. Full report describes 3 CCHD-negative failed screens with neonatal pulmonary hypertension; remaining failed-screen records were affected by algorithm interpretation/reporting issues. Primary denominator is usable with an implementation-error flag; at least 3 actionable CAN-CCHD events among 5 CCHD-negative failures. |
| R088 | Kochilas et al. 2014/2015 — Minnesota reanalysis | COMPANION / NO INDEPENDENT COHORT | Reanalysis of data from the original Minnesota implementation cohort R087 using alternative algorithms. No independent quantitative denominator. |
| R089 | Johnson 2014 — Brigham and Women's well-infant nursery | INCLUDE | 6,838 infants with complete POX data. One infant failed all three screens; echocardiography prompted by screening showed persistent pulmonary hypertension; no CCHD was detected by POX. Primary CAN-CCHD contribution = 1/1 CCHD-negative final failed screen with actionable PPHN. |
| R090 | Wright 2014 — moderate altitude | EXCLUDE PRIMARY / RETAIN THRESHOLD CONTEXT | Approximately 1,000 newborns screened at moderate altitude; study focuses on feasibility and altitude-related false-positive/repeat rates. No reflex echocardiography protocol and no comprehensive diagnosis/outcome/no-diagnosis classification among final CCHD-negative failed screens. Criterion 6 not met. |
| R091 | Gong/Guillory 2017 — Texas Pulse Oximetry Project | EXCLUDE PRIMARY / RETAIN IMPLEMENTATION CONTEXT | 11,322 asymptomatic newborns; 11 positive screens, 1 CCHD and 10 CCHD false positives. Published report provides implementation/QI performance but does not give a complete diagnosis/outcome/management/no-diagnosis classification for the 10 CCHD-negative failed screens. Criterion 6 not met. |
| R092 | Lightfoot 2017/2018 — healthy-newborn audit | EXCLUDE PRIMARY / RETAIN IMPLEMENTATION CONTEXT | 724 screened; 4 failed and were reviewed by a pediatrician; none underwent echocardiography or had CCHD. The report does not identify alternative diagnoses or explicitly classify the four as no-actionable-diagnosis/healthy. Pediatric review alone is insufficient to derive the primary CAN-CCHD numerator. |
| R093 | Cawsey 2016 — homebirths | INCLUDE | 90 homebirth infants underwent pulse-oximetry screening; 2 screened positive and both required neonatal-unit admission with significant respiratory illness; no CCHD. Direct primary contribution = 2/2 CCHD-negative failed screens with actionable respiratory disease. |
| R094 | Schena 2017 — POX + perfusion index | EXCLUDE PRIMARY / RETAIN METHOD CONTEXT | 42,169 asymptomatic newborns, NICU excluded, underwent combined POX+PI screening. Screening identified 3 severe CHD (2 via low SpO2, 1 via low PI), but the report is designed around severe-CHD detection and does not provide a clinically classified denominator of CCHD-negative POX failed screens. Combined positivity further prevents a clean CAN-CCHD POX extraction. |
| R095 | Siefkes 2020 — enhanced CCHD screening method development | EXCLUDE PRIMARY | Artificially assembled method-development sample of healthy newborns and known CHD cases, not a routine/unselected screening cohort. |
| R096 | Sneeringer 2022 — high-altitude threshold study | EXCLUDE PRIMARY / RETAIN THRESHOLD CONTEXT | Retrospective threshold-comparison cohort of 948 newborns. Primary endpoint is first-screen pass/repeat frequency under alternative altitude thresholds. The study explicitly could not determine overall false-positive rates under the standard algorithm because repeat measurements were not consistently performed, and it does not provide diagnoses/outcomes among final CCHD-negative failed screens. Criterion 6 not met. |

## Cohort / QA notes
- R087 and R088 form one Minnesota quantitative cohort; R088 is companion/reanalysis only.
- R089 is especially clean for the review question: the sole final failed screen was CCHD-negative and had PPHN, yielding 1 actionable event / 1 eligible denominator.
- R093 is similarly clean: two positive homebirth screens, both with significant respiratory illness and no CCHD.
- R091 illustrates the strict Phase 4 QA rule: 10 false positives are calculable, but without clinical classification they cannot enter the primary CAN-CCHD proportion.
- R094 and R096 should remain available for methodological discussion/sensitivity context but not pooled in the primary meta-analysis.
